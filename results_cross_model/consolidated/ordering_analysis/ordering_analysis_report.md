## Suggested paper sentence

Across all evaluated model families, direct mention produced the highest mean probe scores, indirect suppression produced intermediate scores, and concept-absent prompts produced the lowest scores. Paired sign-flip permutation tests confirmed both Mention $>$ Suppression$_{indirect}$ and Suppression$_{indirect}$ $>$ Absent for each model.
